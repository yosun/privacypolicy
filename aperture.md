# APERTURE Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

APERTURE is published by Yosun Chang. Its AI3D cloud generation service is operated by AIMagical, Inc, 2021 Fillmore St, San Francisco, CA 94115. Contact [yc@aimagical.com](mailto:yc@aimagical.com) for support, access, correction, or deletion requests.

## Photos and 3D generation

APERTURE asks for permission before uploading your selected photo to AI3D. AI3D uses Google Cloud and processes the photo with TripoSplat through fal to generate a 3D space. Do not submit photos you do not have permission to process. APERTURE does not automatically upload your photo library. Camera access is used for taking a photo and AR placement; live AR camera frames are not uploaded by APERTURE.

AI3D records an installation reference, generation/job identifiers, selected model, uploaded photo, generated model, and technical information needed for authentication, delivery, and abuse prevention. App Attest verifies the app installation, not your personal identity. This version does not publish mobile generation jobs through AI3D's public viewer or offer a public sharing feature. Provider-accessible input links are used to process the photo; do not treat them as public-sharing links.

## Purchases

Purchases use Apple's in-app purchase system and RevenueCat. APERTURE supplies a random, device-specific customer reference to RevenueCat. RevenueCat handles purchase identifiers, product, purchase date, price/storefront and transaction status for purchase verification, app functionality, and purchase analytics. APERTURE does not receive your payment-card details or require an app account, email address, or password to generate a space.

One purchased credit pays for one successful generation. Credits do not expire. The credit balance and consumption records are stored in this iPhone's Keychain, not an account-based cross-device wallet. A definitive failed generation releases its reserved credit; interrupted requests retain their reservation so the same request can be retried. A device wipe or loss of the saved customer reference can require support to investigate remaining credits. This version has no subscription, credit donation, ads, or cross-app advertising tracking.

## Storage and retention

Before completion, APERTURE keeps a protected local copy of the selected photo and pending-job details so an interrupted request can be retried. The pending local photo is removed after a successful download or definitive failure. Downloaded spaces are kept in the app's temporary storage and can be cleared by iOS. Purchase records and the credit balance are retained in Keychain to avoid duplicate credit grants or spending. Non-secret installation/job references are retained locally to help locate service data when requesting deletion.

AI3D's temporary upload objects have an automatic deletion rule after two days. This does **not** delete the separate source-image copy archived with a generation job. Archived source copies, generated models, job/security records, and billing records have no fixed automatic deletion period in the current service and are retained to operate the service, resolve requests/disputes, and meet legal obligations. You can request their deletion at yc@aimagical.com. Required billing/security records may be retained. Deleted Google Cloud objects can remain in recoverable soft-deleted storage for seven days. Provider copies and records are subject to the provider's own retention and deletion practices; this app does not promise instant or simultaneous deletion across providers.

## Your choices and deletion

You can decline cloud photo processing and refrain from uploading. In **Privacy & Data**, choose **Delete Local Photo & Space** to remove the currently selected local photo and downloaded space and reset photo-processing consent. This does not delete the original photo in your library, cloud copies, credits, or purchase records. A pending request must be finished first or resolved with support.

Choose **Prepare Deletion Request Email** in **Privacy & Data** to prepare a request to yc@aimagical.com with the saved RevenueCat customer and AI3D installation/job references. Review and send the email yourself; opening the email does not perform cloud deletion. If Mail is unavailable, copy the displayed references into a message to that address. We may need additional information to locate data and verify the request. Support handles service deletion requests and explains records that must be retained. Deleting the app alone does not request cloud deletion and may not remove Keychain purchase records.

Messages you voluntarily send to support contain the information you include. Support uses them to handle your request. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal information.
