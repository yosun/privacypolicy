# APERTURE Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

APERTURE is published by Yosun Chang. Its AI3D cloud generation service is operated by AIMagical, Inc, 2021 Fillmore St, San Francisco, CA 94115. Contact [yc@aimagical.com](mailto:yc@aimagical.com) for support, access, correction, or deletion requests.

## Photo and text-based world creation

APERTURE asks for permission before cloud processing. For a selected photo, Gemma 4 E2B first analyzes the image on this iPhone. It identifies and crops a dominant character, animal, or distinct object for TripoSplat; a place or environment goes to World Labs Marble Draft with the full photo. Gemma runs locally after its one-time model download; image analysis does not upload the photo. AI3D receives the subject crop or full photo only after you allow cloud creation. A quick preview is created on this iPhone when possible. APERTURE does not automatically upload your photo library. Camera access is used for taking a photo and AR placement; live AR camera frames are not uploaded by APERTURE. Optional **Breath Input** uses the microphone only while enabled in Magical 3D. Live audio levels are analyzed on this iPhone to control the iris; audio is not saved or sent to APERTURE or another service. If you explicitly choose a custom photo style, AI3D also sends the submitted image and style description for a separate FLUX image edit before 3D generation.

**Remove people** is a separate, optional image edit through AI3D's first-party service. The selected photo is processed to identify and remove visible people and return a cleaned copy; your original remains unchanged. The generated image receives a permanent public link. **Create a rigged character** sends the selected photo through AI3D to Tripo P1 for image-to-3D, then to AI3D auto-rig. The P1 intermediate is not published. The completed rigged GLB is published with a permanent public link and uses three purchased credits, even if the first-free-world offer is unused.

If you have no photo, you can plan a world in the on-device Gemma chat. When you approve cloud processing, APERTURE sends your world description through AI3D to World Labs, which generates the 3D world directly. The Gemma assistant's replies stay on this iPhone. The app checks current model availability and the current AI3D reservation estimate before submitting the job. Marble Draft uses one credit (or the first-generation offer, if still available); Marble 1.1 uses four purchased credits and does not consume the free offer. At the listed US $0.49 price per credit, Marble 1.1 uses four credits ($1.96 before any applicable Apple-converted regional pricing).

AI3D records an installation reference, generation/job identifiers, selected model, uploaded photo or world-planning messages, generated outputs, and technical information needed for authentication, delivery, and abuse prevention. For a Marble request, World Labs' privacy policy explains its handling of submitted photos and prompts, including model-development use and applicable opt-out terms. Review [World Labs' Privacy Policy](https://www.worldlabs.ai/privacy-policy) before sharing content that may be routed to Marble. App Attest verifies the app installation, not your personal identity. APERTURE publishes user-facing cloud generation results—including generated images and worlds, finished movies, and meshes—to permanent public hosting at [go.aperture.buzz](https://go.aperture.buzz) by default. Anyone with a result's link can view or download that output and its basic model and generation-time information. For photo-based 3D model results, the public viewer may also show the submitted source image; only continue with photos you have permission to share. Finished movie pages do not show the source or ending screenshots. The hosted page does not include billing details or private job details. The P1 stage used only to create a rigged character and on-device previews are not published. Provider-accessible inputs are used to process the request; their appearance on a hosted page depends on the generated result type as described above.

## Purchases

Purchases use Apple's in-app purchase system and RevenueCat. APERTURE supplies a random, device-specific customer reference to RevenueCat. RevenueCat handles purchase identifiers, product, purchase date, price/storefront and transaction status for purchase verification, app functionality, and purchase analytics. APERTURE does not receive your payment-card details or require an app account, email address, or password to generate a space.

The first successful TripoSplat or Marble Draft world is free if the offer has not already been used. Later TripoSplat object generations and Marble Draft scene generations use one purchased credit; Marble 1.1 text-created worlds use four; a rigged character uses three. Credits do not expire. The credit balance and consumption records are stored in this iPhone's Keychain, not an account-based cross-device wallet. A definitive failed generation releases its reserved credits; interrupted requests retain their reservation so the same request can be retried. A device wipe or loss of the saved customer reference can require support to investigate remaining credits. This version has no subscription, credit donation, ads, or cross-app advertising tracking.

## Storage and retention

Before completion, APERTURE keeps a protected local copy of the prepared generation image (the subject crop for TripoSplat, or full photo for Marble or rigged-character creation) or pending world description, selected generation model, and pending-job details so an interrupted request can be retried. After successful creation, the pending image and world description are removed. Rigged GLB files are kept in the local APERTURE library and can be removed with local media. If a job definitively fails, the app releases its reserved credits; a text world description stays on this iPhone so you can retry or delete it. If AI3D cannot confirm whether a job was accepted, APERTURE reuses the same saved request and idempotency key instead of creating another job automatically. Downloaded spaces are kept in the app's temporary storage and can be cleared by iOS. Purchase records and the credit balance are retained in Keychain to avoid duplicate credit grants or spending. Non-secret installation/job references are retained locally to help locate service data when requesting deletion.

AI3D's temporary upload objects have an automatic deletion rule after two days. This does **not** delete the separate source-image copy archived with a generation job. Generated outputs are copied to permanent hosted storage without a fixed automatic deletion period. Archived source copies, generated models, job/security records, and billing records are also retained without a fixed automatic deletion period in the current service to operate the service, resolve requests/disputes, and meet legal obligations. You can request deletion at yc@aimagical.com; removing hosted results requires a service deletion request. Required billing/security records may be retained. Deleted Google Cloud objects can remain in recoverable soft-deleted storage for seven days. Provider copies and records are subject to the provider's own retention and deletion practices; this app does not promise instant or simultaneous deletion across providers.

## Your choices and deletion

You can decline cloud photo or text-based world processing and refrain from uploading. In **Privacy & Data**, choose **Delete Local Photo & Space** to remove the current local image, saved world description, downloaded space, and locally saved rigged characters, then reset cloud-processing consent. This does not delete the original photo in your library, cloud copies, credits, or purchase records. A pending request must be finished first or resolved with support.

Choose **Prepare Deletion Request Email** in **Privacy & Data** to prepare a request to yc@aimagical.com with the saved RevenueCat customer and AI3D installation/job references. Review and send the email yourself; opening the email does not perform cloud deletion. If Mail is unavailable, copy the displayed references into a message to that address. We may need additional information to locate data and verify the request. Support handles service deletion requests and explains records that must be retained. Deleting the app alone does not request cloud deletion and may not remove Keychain purchase records.

Messages you voluntarily send to support contain the information you include. Support uses them to handle your request. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal information.
